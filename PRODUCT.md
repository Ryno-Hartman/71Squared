# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is South African commercial property decision-makers, including developers, project managers, facilities teams, procurement teams, and operators of malls, retail centres, offices, and other large commercial sites. The website is not aimed at private homeowners or residential projects.

## Product Purpose

This first version is a client-facing website mockup that demonstrates how 71Squared can present itself online at a premium commercial level. It should help the company grow by clearly showing what each specialist division does, displaying relevant work within that division, communicating where the company operates once those details are available, and making it easy for prospective commercial clients to get in contact.

## Positioning

71Squared is one parent company connecting Construction, Flooring, and Plumbing. Each discipline should feel independently credible while sharing one dominant, high-standard commercial identity.

## Conversion & proof

- The primary action is to contact 71Squared through a simple, accessible enquiry route.
- The secondary action is to explore a specialist division and view its related projects.
- The line visitors should remember is: "We build the spaces where business moves forward."
- The belief sequence is: 71Squared understands commercial-scale work; each division has specialist capability; all three divisions share one professional standard; the company is credible and easy to approach.
- No verified testimonials, client logos, certifications, project details, location, or service-area information are available yet. The mockup may use clearly identified South African sample content that must be replaced before public launch.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, minimal, and dominant. The experience should feel established, controlled, precise, and commercially capable rather than whimsical, domestic, or small-business-like. Mobile is the priority, and purposeful motion should make the experience feel active and high-end rather than static.

## Anti-references

Avoid residential-contractor styling, playful or whimsical visuals, generic small-business templates, crowded one-page layouts, excessive decoration, and motion that feels gimmicky. The site must not imply unverified accreditations, clients, or completed projects.

## Design Principles

1. Present three true specialists, not a generic list of services.
2. Make commercial scale visible through composition, language, imagery, and project presentation.
3. Use motion to communicate confidence and structure, never distraction.
4. Prioritise the mobile experience while preserving a commanding desktop presence.
5. Keep the temporary identity modular so official logos, colours, projects, and company details can replace it cleanly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a strong baseline: readable contrast, scalable type, generous mobile touch targets, semantic navigation, keyboard support, visible focus states, meaningful alternative text, and a reduced-motion experience that preserves all content and functionality.
