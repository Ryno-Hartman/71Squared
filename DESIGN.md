# 71Squared Design Direction

## Approved direction

Sequence: a cinematic, mobile-first experience built around three distinct commercial chapters. The interface should feel active, controlled, and ambitious, with image-led sections, oversized typography, editorial spacing, and purposeful scroll motion.

## Temporary identity

The current 71² parent mark and division lockups are intentionally code-based placeholders. They must remain easy to replace with the graphic designer's final identity.

## Colour tokens

- Obsidian: `#0B0C0D`
- Architectural White: `#F4F4F1`
- Graphite: `#2A2B2D`
- Construction / Mineral Orange: `#D85A2A`
- Flooring / Acid Moss: `#A9C93A`
- Plumbing / Cobalt: `#275DFF`

## Motion

Use clipped reveals, restrained image scale, sticky chapters, and short interface transitions. Every motion treatment must have a reduced-motion equivalent with no loss of content.

## Content status

All projects, testimonials, contact details, and locations in this mockup are samples or placeholders until 71Squared supplies verified information.
